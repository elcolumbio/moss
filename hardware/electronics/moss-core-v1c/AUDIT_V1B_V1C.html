V1B met its connectivity rules, but several integration choices were insufficient. V1C revises the mechanical interface, power system, sensitive signals and software contract.

Correction to the previous delivery. A clean ERC/DRC did not establish that the board was ready to manufacture. Claimed alignment with the old pillars was unproven: placement of the enlarged box shifted the holes by 10 mm in rover coordinates. The envelope check had tested neither fasteners nor mated plugs.

Electrical corrections
beyond DRC

1. Control USB current and external rails

Circuit correctedHardware testA “0.5 A hold” PPTC was not a 500 mA cutoff. TPS2553 with 60.4 kohm now defines a calculated 387–491 mA range. Encoders and all four pull-ups switch together: switching only their supply would leave signal back-power paths. The watchdog pull-up changes from 1 kohm to 47 kohm, drawing about 70 uA when held low.

TI datasheets: TPS2553 current setting · TPS22918 encoder rail · TPS3431.

2. Efficiency and restart margin

Circuit correctedHardware testTLV62569 replaces the LDO to reduce losses. The logic-rail design budget is 200 mA typical target / 350 mA continuous, radio off; the buck's 2 A rating is not available from USB. A 464 kohm / 100 kohm, 0.1% divider targets 3.384 V. TLV803E supervises RESET and the disarm chain. Static release margin increased from about 10 to 122 mV, excluding ripple and transients.

TLV62569 · TLV803E. Fast dips and pre-enumeration/suspend current still need measurement; the limiter does not replace USB power policy.

3. Servo: follow the known interface, remove extra circuitry

Circuit correctedThe 5 V servo stage used a quad buffer and a transistor inverter for one output. U14 becomes a 3.3 V SN74LVC1G126, matching the DATA stage of the Waveshare (A) adapter in the rover BOM. Its active-high enable connects directly to hardware permission; three resistors and the transistor are removed. The earlier isolation claim based only on LV4T125 Ioff was too broad.

Official Waveshare schematic · LVC1G126 levels and Ioff. Feetech turnaround timing and the actual harness remain to test.

4. Audio: remove unintended supply paths

Circuit correctedThe AMP_5V→GPIO divider was removed: it applied voltage to the MCU while Core was off. All three I2S inputs now have a defined low idle state. The SD_MODE resistor changes from 47 ohm to 2.2 kohm to limit injection during amplifier power ramps; unlike BCLK/LRCLK/DIN, this input is limited by amplifier supply voltage.

MAX98357A limits and SD_MODE.

5. Read the permissions actually held by the latches

Circuit correctedSoftware port requiredGPIO44 and GPIO1 now read both U9 outputs, allowing firmware to distinguish a transient fault from a software request. Two HCS21 Schmitt gates combine conditions and NC diagnostics; an LVC receiver preserves external fault-input tolerance. The latches remain.

HCS21 · LVC74. None of these gates alone disconnects arm power.

USB is a routing constraint, not two arbitrary connections

Routing correctedStatically checkedD+ and D− now remain on F.Cu without signal vias. Total copper length is 16.292 / 16.119 mm including reversible-connector branches; this is not propagation delay per plug orientation. After the 22 ohm resistors, both paths measure 1.84 mm. Both internal layers are GND planes without signal traces. The masked 2D model estimates 88–90 ohm for the nominal 0.15 / 0.15 mm section; the manufacturer must confirm stackup, etching and impedance.

ESP32-S3 guide · JLCPCB stackup and mask data · reproducible calculation: USB_IMPEDANCE_REVIEW.txt. This does not replace a coupon or USB test.

Why “ERC = 0 / DRC = 0” was not enough

The V1B results were real. They checked project-defined rules and connection consistency. They did not check screw volumes, pillar alignment, USB impedance, power return paths, endpoint budgets or MOSS software behavior.

Logic combinations following exported physical nets and pins.

Fault/recovery sequences and sensitivity test with a modified logic-gate pin.

These models assume stable power and valid logic levels. They simulate neither ramps, metastability nor firmware. The combined schematic passed ERC. Final DRC: 0 violations, 0 unconnected items, 0 parity issues. JSON reports identify ignored checks and the reviewed files. These results do not validate installation in the rover.

Software port required to retain rover functions

Software port requiredThe existing motor client automatically rearms once after ERR not_armed. Hardware faults therefore need a distinct state and code, with explicit acknowledgement. QT Py firmware does not control the new permissions and its INA219 driver does not suit INA226.

To retain system audio and TTS, the selected profile is one CDC ACM + UAC playback with feedback: five endpoints excluding EP0, within the S3 limit. A broker will expose two pseudo-TTYs for motors and servos. Two standard CDCs plus UAC would exceed the budget. This electronics package alone supplies no operational broker or firmware.

Espressif USB stack and limits · Espressif CDC budget · local code: moss_adapter.py::_drive, sounds.py and QT Py firmware.

Tests and decisions still required

  1. Manufacturing: static routing checks passed. Confirm stackup, impedance and assembly with the manufacturer; repeat checks after changes. No production cost has been validated.
  2. Power and USB: pre-enumeration, suspend/resume, hot/cold starts, rail collapse, current and closed-hull temperature. Supervision does not guarantee reset before 3.0 V for every fast collapse.
  3. Actual control: stalled watchdog, reset, NC, external fault, return to healthy conditions without automatic restart, continuous audio and loaded Feetech bus. Audio/servo traffic must never refresh the motor timeout.
  4. Power wiring and mechanics: Kelvin shunt and sense-wire protection, external cutoff, arm behavior without commands, mated plugs, cover and integrated printed support.
Keep this limit explicit: blocking servo TX does not stop a trajectory already received. J9 remains a logic permission, not a power switch. Removing modules does not establish a cost saving either: PCB, assembly, connectors, harnesses and external cutoff must be included in the quotation.
