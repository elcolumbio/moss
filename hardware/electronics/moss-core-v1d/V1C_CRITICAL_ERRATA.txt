MOSS Core V1C — critical physical-pin mapping erratum
Date: 2026-10-15
Status: DO NOT FABRICATE THE V1C PACKAGE.

The V1C physical-pin contract incorrectly assigns channel 2 of SN74LVC2G08DCUR
in U10 and U11. Its second AND gate is physically inputs 5 and 6, output 3.
The V1C contract instead uses inputs 3 and 5, output 6. Correct logical equations
and a clean ERC/DRC cannot detect an incorrect custom-symbol pin contract.

Consequences include an invalid actuator-permission path and invalid second
motor PWM mask. The previously published V1C package is not a functional or
fabrication-ready prototype. Do not treat a previous ERC/DRC pass as validation.

This was independently identified against TI SCES198N, section 5, page 3:
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g08.pdf

V1D corrects U10 and replaces U11 with a four-channel mask that also inhibits
DIR signals when motor power is absent. The new servo-stage U13 is checked
against the same physical pin table. Recorded independent physical-pin review reports accompany V1D.

The historical V1C archive is preserved unchanged for traceability. This
erratum accompanies the public V1D routed DRAFT. V1D routing is complete
with the recorded native DRC/netlist checks passing. Geometric review,
manufacturing qualification and hardware validation remain pending.
