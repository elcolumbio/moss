MOSS Core V1D - connector guide
Working design; not a manufacturing or powered-test release.
Pin numbers follow the native PCB pads, not wire colors or a cable photograph.

J1 - USB-C DEVICE
Connect to the host computer or its external USB hub. Core is a USB peripheral.
It does not host cameras or provide the host computer's power supply.

J10 - FUSED 3S BATTERY INPUT
1: battery positive, after the external fuse. 2: battery negative / GND.
This feeds the on-board actuator switches and the separately fused audio buck.
Battery/BMS regenerative-current behavior still requires qualification.

J11 - CYTRON POWER OUTPUT
1: switched motor positive. 2: power GND.
This supplies the external Cytron MDD10A. It is separate from J2 logic.
The branch has an approximately 8.1 A nominal TOTAL budget, not 10 A per motor.

J2 - CYTRON CONTROL SIGNALS
1: PWM1. 2: DIR1. 3: PWM2. 4: DIR2. 5: GND.
No motor power goes through this connector. Cable order is Core-specific.

J3 / J4 - LEFT / RIGHT MOTOR ENCODERS
1: GND. 2: switched encoder 3V3. 3: channel A. 4: channel B.
The physical motor cable pin order must be matched to these PCB pin numbers.

J5 - POWERED SERIAL SERVO CHAIN
1: serial DATA. 2: switched battery positive. 3: GND.
One existing chain is retained. Proposed current limit is approximately
2.5 A nominal; this is not a guarantee that every arm joint can stall together.
The selected 2.5 mm connector still requires a physical cable mating check.

J7 - SPEAKER
1: speaker +. 2: speaker -. Intended speaker: 4 ohm, 3 W, two-pin 2 mm pitch.
Both leads are driven by the amplifier: neither is GND. Output power limiting
still requires firmware implementation and measurement into a dummy load.

J8 - NORMALLY CLOSED STOP CONTACT
1: NC_SOURCE. 2: NC_RETURN.
The external normally closed contact joins these pins during permitted operation.
Opening the contact or its wire inhibits the actuator-power hardware.
This is a low-voltage dry-contact loop, not a battery-current connection.
The host computer retains its separate supply when actuator power is removed.
This prototype is not a certified safety controller.

J9 - OPTIONAL EXTERNAL LOGIC INTERFACE
1: GND. 2: PERMIT output, 3V3 logic. 3: active-low external FAULT input.
Pin 2 reports the final actuator permit through R21; it is not an enable input
and must not be driven by another output. Pin 3 can request an inhibit by
pulling it low; R18 pulls the filtered fault signal high when it is unused.
J9 is the retained external interface. The V1D power switches are on the PCB,
so a separate cut-off board on J9 is not required for the integrated function.
Do not bridge J9 pins or connect battery voltage here.

J6 - KELVIN MEASUREMENT INPUT
1: K+. 2: K-. 3: GND. 4: reserved / not connected.
This is a measurement connection, not an alternative battery-power input.
The external measurement wiring must follow the current-sense schematic.

Physical checks still required
Confirm each mating housing, pin order, polarity, wire rating and installed
clearance before the first powered prototype. See the schematic and firmware
porting contract for the complete startup and fault-reset requirements.
